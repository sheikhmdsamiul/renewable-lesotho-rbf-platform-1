## 3. Role-Based Functionalities

### 3.1 Platform Administrator (Super Admin)
**Primary Responsibility:** System governance, user management, and technical configuration.

*   **User Management:** Create, edit, and deactivate system users. Reset passwords for any user.
*   **Organization Management:** Manage the registry of government, NGO, and international organizations.
*   **System Configuration:** Set global platform targets (e.g., female inclusion %), GPS verification thresholds, and currency settings.
*   **Prospect Sync Control:** Monitor the real-time data synchronization with the Prospect (external) system. Retry failed sync jobs and test API connections.
*   **System Audit Logs:** Access the full historical trail of all actions taken on the platform. Export logs to CSV or PDF for compliance.
*   **System Health:** Monitor server uptime, database status, and API performance.

### 3.2 RBF Management Team (RMT)
**Primary Responsibility:** Operational management of tenders, projects, and payments.

*   **Tender Lifecycle Management:** Create new tenders, verify tender information, publish to vendors, and manage the awarding process.
*   **Project Oversight:** Approve project setups, track milestones, and review project documentation uploaded by vendors.
*   **Contracting (PBA):** Generate Performance-Based Agreements (PBA), review vendor signatures, and finalize contracts.
*   **Payment Claim Processing:** Perform a multi-stage review of payment claims (Verify -> Approve -> Finalize).
*   **Vendor Prequalification:** Review registration documents and approve/reject vendor applications to the platform.
*   **Blacklisting:** Initiate, review, and confirm blacklisting cases for non-compliant vendors.
*   **Issue Resolution:** Respond to concerns raised by DoE officers and findings raised by Auditors.
*   **Notice Management:** Create and publish system-wide announcements and notices.

### 3.3 TAC Member (Technical Advisory Committee)
**Primary Responsibility:** Technical evaluation and endorsement.

*   **Bid Evaluation:** Review and score technical bids submitted by vendors during tender stages.
*   **Claim Endorsement:** Technically verify and endorse payment claims before they move to final RMT approval.
*   **Technical Dashboard:** View a curated list of items awaiting technical review.
*   **Reports:** Access technical performance reports through the Reports Hub.

### 3.4 DoE Officer (Department of Energy)
**Primary Responsibility:** Regional monitoring and community-level oversight.

*   **Regional Dashboard:** Monitor projects specifically assigned to their districts (e.g., Maseru, Leribe).
*   **Raise Concerns:** Flag project-level issues (e.g., poor installation quality, KPI risks) directly to the RMT or PSC.
*   **Regional GIS Map:** View a map of all installations within their region (read-only).
*   **Regional KPI Monitoring:** Track regional progress against national targets.

### 3.5 Project Steering Committee (PSC)
**Primary Responsibility:** Strategic oversight and escalation management.

*   **Macro KPI Portal:** High-level dashboard showing national progress, inclusion compliance (female/vulnerable), and payment pacing.
*   **Escalation Oversight:** View serious concerns raised by DoE officers or major findings from Auditors that have been escalated for high-level attention.
*   **PSC Directives:** Add comments or directives to escalated issues, instructing the RMT on required actions.
*   **Resolution Power:** Mark escalated issues as resolved once satisfied with the RMT's investigation.

### 3.6 Auditor
**Primary Responsibility:** Compliance verification and fraud detection.

*   **Auditor Dashboard:** Summary of system compliance, anomaly statistics, and failed syncs.
*   **Full GIS & KPI Access:** Read-only access to the entire portfolio's geographic and performance data.
*   **Anomaly Reporting:** Automated and manual flagging of suspicious patterns (e.g., GPS fraud, KPI manipulation).
*   **Audit Findings:** Formalize "Findings" (Observation to Critical), recommend actions, and track RMT responses.
*   **Full System Audit Trail:** Unrestricted access to system logs and Prospect sync activity for data integrity verification.

### 3.7 Field Verifier
**Primary Responsibility:** Physical verification of installations and field-level data integrity.

*   **Field Inspections:** Access a queue of verification tasks assigned to their specific district/zone.
*   **Mobile-Optimized Verification:** Perform multi-step site inspections, including capturing real-time GPS coordinates, verifying equipment serial numbers, and documenting system functionality.
*   **Inspection Outcomes:** Mark installations as "Verified," "Flagged" (for issues), or "Partial" based on field reality.
*   **Regional GIS Mapping:** View a map of assigned sites to plan verification routes and locate remote installations.
*   **Activity & History:** Track personal verification performance and historical activity across assigned projects.
*   **Task Notifications:** Receive alerts when new installations are ready for field verification.

## 4. Common Portal Features
*   **Notifications:** All officials receive real-time alerts for tasks requiring their attention.
*   **Reports Hub:** A centralized location to generate and download various reports (Excel/PDF).
*   **Impact Portal:** Access to the public-facing dashboard showing the overall social and environmental impact of the RBF program.
